## Facts the terms must match

Read from the code on 8 Oct 2026 (`e577896`). If the draft says something
these don't support, the draft is wrong or the code needs checking first.

- **Who:** Sshubhan Kammari, ksshubhan@gmail.com (as in `docs/privacy.md`).
- **What it makes:** generated practice papers and worksheets, each with its
  mark scheme. Edexcel GCSE Maths, Foundation and Higher. Questions are
  generated by code (sympy), not taken from real past papers, and no AI model
  is involved. Every PDF says "Not affiliated with or endorsed by Pearson
  Education Ltd."
- **Who holds the account:** `/pricing` says a parent or guardian, an adult.
  Nothing in the code checks age.
- **Free plan:** 1 paper or worksheet in total (`FREE_GENERATION_LIMIT=1`).
- **Unlimited:** £5 a month (`PRICE_MONTHLY_DISPLAY`), Stripe subscription,
  renews monthly until cancelled. Fair use: 10 papers or worksheets per
  Europe/London calendar day (`DAILY_GENERATION_LIMIT=10`).
- **Abuse ceilings on every plan, per London day:** 20 generations started
  (`MAX_DAILY_ATTEMPTS`), 50 PDF downloads (`MAX_DAILY_RENDERS`).
  Re-downloading a paper costs no paper. A generation that fails is given back.
- **Cancelling:** `/account` → Manage subscription → Stripe Customer Portal.
  Cancels at the end of the paid period; Unlimited stays until then and
  `/account` shows the cancel date. The portal also changes the card.
- **Refunds:** nothing in the code issues one. A refund is by hand in the
  Stripe dashboard.
- **Checkout asks for nothing beyond payment:** no terms acceptance and no
  cooling-off waiver is collected. (Relevant if the draft mentions the 14-day
  right to cancel for digital content.)
- **Each PDF is stamped** "Generated for {account email} · {date}" in its
  footer.
- **Papers are not stored:** topics and paper content are not saved; a paper
  is downloaded straight after generating.
- **Account deletion:** by email request, by hand (per `docs/privacy.md`).
  No self-serve delete. Nothing in the code suspends or bans an account.

## Topics the draft must cover

Wording is Sshubhan's. These are what UK consumer terms for a paid digital
service usually cover; skip one only on purpose.

1. Who provides ExamPaper and how to contact them.
2. What the service is: generated papers in the exam board's style, not real
   past papers, not affiliated with Pearson, no promise about grades.
3. Accounts: who should hold one, keeping sign-in secure.
4. Plans and price: Free, Unlimited, fair use.
5. Billing, renewal and cancelling, including what happens at the end of the
   period and how a price change would be notified.
6. Refunds and the right to cancel (Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013).
7. Acceptable use: no scripts or scraping, no getting round the limits.
8. No redistribution: what the user may and may not do with generated papers
   (own household? a tutor's own students? never resold or published).
9. Ownership of the site and the generated content.
10. Availability and changes to the service.
11. Liability, including that consumer rights under the Consumer Rights Act
    2015 are not affected.
12. Suspending or ending an account, and deletion (link to `/privacy`).
13. Governing law: England and Wales.
14. Changes to these terms, and a "Last updated" date.
